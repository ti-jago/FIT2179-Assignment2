Where Visitors Come From

Australia's visitors largely arrive from the Asia-Pacific region. New Zealand sends the most trips, while China contributes the highest total spending by a visiting nation. The map below shows the global distribution.

Spend, Stay, and Volume

Trip numbers alone don't show the full picture economically. Chinese visitors spend nearly four times more than New Zealanders, despite similar (slightly less) trip counts. Indian visitors stay the longest on average, while Japanese tourists spend more per night.

Which Markets Are Growing?

Comparing 2024 to 2025 forecasts reveals which source markets are expanding fastest. The United Kingdom, China, and Hong Kong are among the strongest growers, while Korea and Indonesia show slight declines. Understanding these trends helps Australia's tourism industry plan ahead.
